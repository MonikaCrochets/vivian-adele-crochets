Policies & Customer Guidelines

These policies explain the terms for custom orders, ready-made purchases, shipping, returns, refunds, and crochet lessons at Vivian Adele's Crochet.

1. Custom Orders, Pricing & Payment

Deposits and payment

  • A 25% deposit of the total item price is required for all custom orders before production begins.
  • The deposit is applied toward the total cost of the item.
  • Deposits are non-refundable except when a customer cancels within 24 hours of placing the order, subject to applicable law.
  • The remaining balance is due upon receipt of the order confirmation email. Production begins once the required selections are finalized and payment requirements have been met.

Customization and pricing

  • Custom orders may include color and style selections, which may affect the final price.
  • Custom sizing for larger dimensions is not available on any item.
  • All color and style choices must be confirmed by email before production begins.
  • Once production has started, major changes may incur additional charges. Any additional charges will be communicated to the customer for approval before the changes are made.

What counts as a major change?

A major color change includes removing, adding, or replacing any color in the original order.

A major style change depends on the nature and complexity of the requested alteration. For example:

  • Not generally a major change: Adding a simple border to a baby blanket.
  • A major change: Changing a simple striped blanket design to a granny square blanket.

If you request a style change after production has begun, we will discuss the request with you to determine whether it is a major change and whether additional charges or production time will apply.

Production timeline

Custom handmade items require a minimum turnaround time of four weeks before delivery or shipping. Actual completion times may vary depending on the item's complexity and any approved changes. Any shipping-date commitments and delay notices will be handled in accordance with applicable law.

2. Processing & Shipping

Ready-made items and processing time

Please allow up to seven business days for processing on ready-made items. Processing time begins when your order is confirmed and ends when your package is dropped off with USPS. The seven-business-day processing period does not include USPS transit time. Delivery times depend on the shipping service and destination and are outside our direct control once a package has been handed to USPS.

Payment for ready-made items is due in full at the time of purchase.

Shipping methods and costs

  • All shipped orders are sent through USPS.
  • Customers are responsible for shipping costs displayed or communicated before payment.
  • After your package is dropped off with USPS, we will email you the tracking information, where available.
  • Local pickup details will be provided for orders arranged for pickup.

Shipping delays

Vivian Adele's Crochet will notify customers by email of delays that are our responsibility and will provide an updated estimated shipping date when reasonably available.

If a delay attributable to Vivian Adele's Crochet extends to three or more days beyond the promised shipping date, we will provide a goodwill refund equal to 50% of the remaining item balance after subtracting any custom-order deposit.

For example, for a $100 custom order with a $25 deposit, the remaining item balance is $75, so the goodwill refund would be $37.50. For ready-made items without a deposit, the goodwill refund would be 50% of the item price.

This goodwill refund does not limit any other rights the customer may have under applicable law. If we cannot ship an order within the promised timeframe, we will provide any required delay notice and cancellation or refund options under applicable law. A customer who chooses to cancel under those rules will receive the refund required by law, which may be a full refund of all amounts paid for the unshipped merchandise.

3. Refunds, Cancellations & Returns

Custom-order cancellations

  • Cancellation within 24 hours: The customer will receive a full refund of the amount paid for the order, including the deposit.
  • Cancellation after 24 hours but before completion: The 25% deposit is retained, and any remaining amount paid toward the item price will be refunded.
  • Cancellation after the custom item is complete: The item price is non-refundable. Only the shipping cost will be refunded, subject to applicable law.

For a custom order with a $100 item price and a $25 deposit:

Cancellation timingItem-price refund
Within 24 hours of placing the orderFull refund of the amount paid (up to $100 item price)
After 24 hours, before completion$75, if the full $100 item price has been paid
After the item is completeItem price is non-refundable; shipping cost only is refundable

Refunds will not exceed the amount actually paid. These terms are subject to any refund or cancellation rights required by applicable law.

Ready-made item cancellations

If you request cancellation before your order ships, you will receive a full refund of the item price and shipping fees paid for that unshipped order, subject to applicable legal requirements. Once an item has shipped, cancellation is no longer available through the pre-shipment cancellation process. You may submit a return request under the policy below.

Ready-made returns

  • Return requests must be submitted within 10 calendar days of delivery. Requests made after this period will not be accepted, except where applicable law requires otherwise.
  • All returns must be approved before an item is sent back.
  • For an approved return of an item that is not incorrect, the item must be returned undamaged.
  • After the returned item is received and inspected, the item price will be refunded in accordance with the approved return.
  • Customers are responsible for return shipping costs for approved returns of items that are not incorrect.
  • Original shipping fees are not refunded for ordinary returns, except where a refund is required by applicable law.

Incorrect items

If you receive an item that does not match your confirmed order, please contact Vivian Adele's Crochet before returning it. We must discuss and confirm the issue and approve the return before the item is sent back.

  • Vivian Adele's Crochet will cover the shipping cost for returning the incorrect item to us.
  • We will correct or replace the item as appropriate.
  • We will cover the shipping cost to send the corrected item back to you.
  • The original shipping fee will not be refunded under this correction-and-reshipment process.
  • This correction-and-reshipment arrangement is a one-time offer per order.

This process does not limit any additional remedy that may be required by applicable law if the issue cannot be resolved through correction or replacement. Please contact us promptly if your item arrives damaged or does not match your confirmed order.

4. Crochet Lessons

Booking and deposits

  • Online/virtual lessons: $10 deposit.
  • In-person lessons: $25 deposit.
  • Lesson deposits are non-refundable except where this policy permits the deposit to be applied to one rescheduled lesson or applicable law requires otherwise.
  • A lesson is confirmed once the booking arrangements have been agreed upon and the required deposit has been received.

Lesson payments

The remaining lesson balance is due at least 24 hours before the scheduled lesson. Customers are responsible for ensuring that the balance is paid by this deadline.

Cancellations and rescheduling

  • Customers should provide at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule a lesson.
  • If a customer cancels with less than 24 hours' notice, the deposit will be retained.
  • If the customer requests a reschedule immediately following a late cancellation, the retained deposit may be applied toward one rescheduled lesson.
  • If a customer misses a lesson without attending or providing notice, the deposit will be retained. Any remaining lesson balance already paid will be refunded.
  • If a lesson must be canceled by Vivian Adele's Crochet, the customer may choose to reschedule or receive a refund for payments made toward the canceled lesson.

Please contact us as soon as possible if an unexpected circumstance affects your ability to attend.

Lesson platforms and materials

  • Virtual lessons will be conducted through Zoom or Google Meet. Customers will receive the necessary meeting details before their scheduled lesson.
  • Basic beginner crochet materials are provided for in-person beginner lessons.
  • Virtual students must have their own crochet hook, yarn, camera, and microphone ready for the session.

5. Contact & Customer Support

For questions about custom orders, color or style selections, order status, shipping, refunds, returns, or crochet lessons, please contact Monika through the website contact form or by email at vivianadelecrochet@mail.com.

Please allow up to three business days for a response to inquiries.

Thank you for supporting Vivian Adele's Crochet. Every handmade creation is prepared with care. These policies are intended to set clear expectations and support a positive customer experience. Nothing in these policies is intended to waive rights that cannot legally be waived.
